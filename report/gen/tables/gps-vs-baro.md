| **Height** | **Measured from** | **Typical error** |
|---|---|---|
| GPS, raw | WGS84 ellipsoid | ±10-30 m vertical, worse indoors |
| Mean sea level | the geoid (60-100 m below the ellipsoid here) | depends on the app's geoid model |
| Pressure altitude (`A`, 1013.25 hPa) | where pressure would be 1013.25 hPa | about 8 m per hPa of weather |
| QNH altitude (`A`, QNH entered) | sea level, via today's QNH | about ±10 m |
